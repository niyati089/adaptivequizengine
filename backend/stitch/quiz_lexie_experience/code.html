Which of the following phrases is most appropriate for politely disagreeing in a formal boardroom setting?

Socratic AI

Thinking...

"Consider the social stakes here, Simona. In formal settings, direct contradiction is often seen as aggressive. How might we maintain respect while expressing a different view?"

lightbulb Hint

Look for 'softeners'—words like 'however' or 'perspective'—that acknowledge the other person's contribution before introducing your own.

menu_book Language Insight

In linguistics, this is called 'Politeness Theory.' By using the phrase "I see your perspective," you are preserving the speaker's 'face' while preparing them for a counter-point.
